- heading "About the Project" [level=2]
- paragraph: Auralis is an end-to-end machine learning and data engineering system for estimating solar activity from NASA SDO/HMI magnetograms.
- paragraph: The platform processes local solar imagery, estimates a global strong-field pixel index, and reports the current activity index with an illustrative sensitivity diagnostic. Current validation is used for checkpoint selection, not an independent or temporal test. Low-activity and border/background XAI limitations remain.
